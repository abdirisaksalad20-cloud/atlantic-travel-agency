ABOUT OUR COMPANY

A Travel Company Built on Experience

Atlantic Travel Agency was established in 2011 and is based in Mogadishu, Somalia. The company serves travelers from Mogadishu and other regions of Somalia, helping them arrange domestic and international journeys.

We focus on customer service, convenient travel arrangements and helping our customers understand the options available for their trips.

Our Mission

To make travel arrangements more accessible and convenient by providing professional assistance, clear information and responsive customer service.

Our Vision

To continue developing as a trusted travel service provider for customers in Somalia and beyond.
